# Data and third-party sources

The MIT license applies to this project’s original software and documentation. It does not grant rights in third-party material, trademarks, publisher descriptions, or source publications.

Rank/date/title/author records were transcribed by Hawes Publications (https://hawes.com/pastlist.htm) from New York Times lists. Source URLs and retrieval metadata are retained. Subject suggestions reference public catalog headings and descriptions; they are not library classifications. Google Books and Chapel Hill Public Library catalog links point to third-party services. This project is independent and unaffiliated with those organizations.

Downloaded PDFs, extracted source prose, and research caches are excluded from this repository. Import scripts can reconstruct the archive from its public sources. The included structured indexes support running the app without those downloads.
